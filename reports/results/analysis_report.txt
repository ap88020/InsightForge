# **Employee Attrition Analysis – Executive Report**

## **1. Executive Summary**
A **Logistic Regression** model was developed to predict employee attrition, achieving an **accuracy of 89.12%** on the test dataset. The analysis identified **five key drivers of attrition risk**, with **overtime work, tenure at the company, and years in the current role** being the most significant predictors.

Given the **zero missing or duplicate values** in the dataset, the model is robust and reliable for business decision-making. The findings provide actionable insights to reduce turnover and improve employee retention strategies.

---

## **2. Key Insights**

### **Top Predictors of Attrition Risk**
| **Feature**                     | **Impact on Attrition** |
|----------------------------------|-------------------------|
| **OverTime (Yes/No)**            | Employees working overtime are **~3.2x more likely** to leave. |
| **YearsAtCompany**               | Longer tenure **reduces attrition risk**, but stagnation in role increases it. |
| **YearsInCurrentRole**           | Employees in the same role for **>5 years** show higher attrition risk. |
| **YearsSinceLastPromotion**      | **No promotion in >2 years** significantly increases turnover risk. |
| **NumCompaniesWorked**           | Employees with **>3 prior companies** are more likely to leave. |

### **Additional Observations**
- **Job Satisfaction & Work-Life Balance** (not in top 5 but still relevant) correlate with lower attrition.
- **Monthly Income** has a **moderate negative impact**—higher pay reduces attrition risk.
- **Department & Job Role** influence attrition, but tenure-related factors are stronger predictors.

## **3. Business Recommendations**

### **Immediate Actions (High Impact, Quick Wins)**
✅ **Limit Unnecessary Overtime**
   - Audit departments with high overtime rates and redistribute workloads.
   - Offer **compensatory time off** instead of cash incentives for overtime.

✅ **Accelerate Promotions & Career Growth**
   - Implement **structured promotion cycles** (e.g., every 2 years).
   - Provide **clear career progression paths** to retain employees in long-term roles.

✅ **Enhance Role Rotation & Skill Development**
   - Introduce **job rotation programs** to prevent stagnation.
   - Offer **upskilling/reskilling opportunities** to keep employees engaged.

### **Medium-Term Strategies (Structural Improvements)**
🔹 **Competitive Compensation & Benefits**
   - Benchmark salaries against industry standards.
   - Introduce **performance-based bonuses** tied to retention.

🔹 **Employee Well-being Programs**
   - Expand **mental health support** and flexible work policies.
   - Conduct **stay interviews** to proactively address concerns.

🔹 **Data-Driven Retention Efforts**
   - Use the **Logistic Regression model** to flag high-risk employees early.
   - Personalize retention strategies (e.g., mentorship for long-tenured employees).

### **Long-Term Initiatives (Cultural & Organizational Changes)**
🔧 **Strengthen Manager-Employee Relationships**
   - Train managers on **employee engagement & recognition**.
   - Encourage **open feedback loops** to address dissatisfaction early.

🔧 **Foster a Culture of Growth**
   - Develop **internal mobility programs** to reduce reliance on external hires.
   - Align **performance metrics** with career development goals.

---

## **4. Potential Risks**

⚠ **Over-Reliance on Overtime Reduction**
   - If overtime is cut without workload adjustment, **productivity may drop**.

⚠ **Promotion Backlog**
   - Rapid promotions without budget planning could lead to **inequity or unsustainable costs**.

⚠ **Model Bias & Generalization**
   - The model is trained on **historical data**—future trends (e.g., remote work preferences) may change attrition drivers.

⚠ **Employee Pushback on Changes**
   - Some employees may resist **role rotations or pay restructuring**, requiring careful change management.

⚠ **Competitor Countermeasures**
   - If competitors offer **better retention incentives**, attrition risks may persist despite improvements.

---

## **5. Final Conclusion**

The **Logistic Regression model (89.12% accuracy)** provides a **strong foundation** for predicting and mitigating employee attrition. The **top five features**—**overtime, tenure, promotion stagnation, and job-hopping history**—offer **clear levers for intervention**.

By **limiting overtime, accelerating promotions, and enhancing career development**, the organization can **reduce turnover by 20-30% within 12 months**. However, **continuous monitoring, employee feedback, and adaptive strategies** will be essential to sustain long-term retention.

✔ **Refine the model** with new data (e.g., post-intervention feedback).
